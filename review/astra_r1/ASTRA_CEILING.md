**Aim for a product people use to challenge a headline, then share the evidence.** The False-Alarm Planet supplies spectacle. The larger opportunity is to make visitors experience how an apparently persuasive climate story gets constructed—and learn to write a better one.

The build has advanced since the 15/25 review: the adapter now selects the intended statistics, and the IMERG Sahel bundle exists. This assessment credits those changes without treating source inspection as completed browser verification.

**1. What caps the ceiling**

- **The courtroom mostly judges numbers, not consequential claims.** Visitors need a recognizable question and a conclusion they can responsibly repeat.
- **Threshold crossing is carrying too much drama.** Moving p from 0.008 to 0.058 does not establish a scientific reversal. Effect size, uncertainty, coverage and assumptions deserve equal visibility.
- **The audit still needs auditing.** Selecting a test from estimated persistence requires validation of that entire procedure. Correct display wiring alone cannot earn Validity 5.
- **The current story conflates different evidence.** The new IMERG Sahel result has p≈0.030; the planned 0.008→0.058 scene belongs to the separate POWER demo. Different products and periods must remain identifiable.
- **Impact is asserted rather than demonstrated.** Another visualization will not show that a student can distinguish an event from a trend, recognize cherry-picking, or improve a misleading headline.

**2. Ten additions that raise the ceiling**

Hours below are incremental **agent-hours**, including focused verification, assuming existing infrastructure. Data acquisition can exceed these estimates.

1. **Headline Clinic: turn a real circulating claim into a measurable question.**  
   **Wow:** paste a headline; highlight its place, variable, period and unsupported causal leap, then receive a narrower, defensible rewrite. Start with three sourced claims and manual structured choices.  
   **Honesty:** evaluate the measurable component; explicitly mark the rest “not tested.” NASA’s [Sahara rainfall explainer](https://gpm.nasa.gov/applications/weather/news/saharaDesertAugust2024) offers a concrete event-versus-trend example.  
   **Data:** POWER T2M/PRECTOTCORR; preprocessed GPM_3IMERGM V07; MYD08_M3.061 AOD.  
   **Awards:** Storytelling, Inspirational, Galactic Impact. **Hours:** 2–3. **Risk:** falsely promising unrestricted automated fact-checking.

2. **“Make the opposite headline”: let visitors manufacture a misleading story, then expose its construction.**  
   **Wow:** your existing Alaska record already supports opposite *descriptive* slopes: OLS gives approximately **−0.53°C/decade for 1998–2012** and **+1.75°C/decade for 2005–2019**. Reveal both selections inside the full record. I calculated these from the [archived regional series](<web/data/bundle_C-C.json>).  
   **Honesty:** these are deliberately selected teaching examples, not significance findings; show all eligible windows and their lengths.  
   **Data:** existing POWER/MERRA-2 T2M.  
   **Awards:** Creativity criterion, Storytelling, Science, Inspirational. **Hours:** 2–3. **Risk:** screenshots circulating without context; permanently watermark “selected window.”

3. **Witness Confrontation: compare products and reveal their shared ancestry.**  
   **Wow:** switch between satellite-derived rainfall and reanalysis; see which conclusions survive changing the witness.  
   **Honesty:** align box, season, years, units and analysis before comparison; retain disagreement. Draw shared-input connections: IMERG Final itself uses gauges and MERRA-2 information, so agreement is not fully independent confirmation. [NASA technical documentation](https://gpm.nasa.gov/sites/default/files/2023-07/IMERG_TechnicalDocumentation_final_230713.pdf)  
   **Data:** existing IMERG V07 versus POWER PRECTOTCORR, Sahel JJAS 2001–2025.  
   **Awards:** Data, Science. **Hours:** 3–4. **Risk:** mistaking processing differences for physical disagreement.

4. **Mechanism Twins: show how opposite signs can tell one physically coherent story.**  
   **Wow:** temperature rises while snow cover falls; visitors connect the two records themselves. Extend later to contrasting regions.  
   **Honesty:** label this “consistent with a mechanism,” not causal attribution; preserve alternative explanations, seasonal alignment and cloud gaps. Never require the expected signs to appear.  
   **Data:** POWER T2M and preprocessed [MOD10CM.061 monthly snow cover](https://doi.org/10.5067/MODIS/MOD10CM.061), one predefined mountain region.  
   **Awards:** Science, Storytelling, Inspirational. **Hours:** 5–7. **Risk:** snow retrieval gaps and unequal spatial support.

5. **Earth Evidence Atlas: make the globe an index of answerable questions.**  
   **Wow:** touch a location and discover which measurements show change, which remain inconclusive, and which lack usable evidence.  
   **Honesty:** distinguish effect, uncertainty and missingness; define testing families in advance; avoid one universal “climate verdict.” Show footprint resolution.  
   **Data:** preprocessed coarse MERRA-2 T2M, IMERG V07 precipitation and MOD11C3.061 daytime LST.  
   **Awards:** Data, Galactic Impact, Art and Technology. **Hours:** 10–16. **Risk:** global processing, QC and multiplicity; unsuitable for the critical path.

6. **Evidence Receipts: make every shared verdict reopenable and challengeable.**  
   **Wow:** a verdict card opens the exact data snapshot and settings; “Challenge this finding” forks the analysis and displays what changed.  
   **Honesty:** include product/version, region, season, period, slope, uncertainty status, limitations, input hash and code version. A hash identifies evidence; it does not certify correctness.  
   **Data:** existing POWER and IMERG bundles.  
   **Awards:** Science, Data, Galactic Impact. **Hours:** 2–3. **Risk:** an attractive image without a working replay link.

7. **Evidence Time Machine: replay what could have been concluded as observations accumulated.**  
   **Wow:** the same question stays on screen while years arrive, uncertainty changes and findings stabilize—or fail to.  
   **Honesty:** call this retrospective sensitivity analysis; repeated threshold crossings are not fresh discoveries. Reprocessed archives cannot reproduce exactly what historical analysts knew.  
   **Data:** existing Alaska T2M and Sahel IMERG series.  
   **Awards:** Storytelling, Science. **Hours:** 2–3. **Risk:** implying longer records guarantee significance.

8. **Mission Planning Lab: replace the promised detection date with an experiment.**  
   **Wow:** choose a scientifically meaningful trend; compare investing in a longer record versus lower measurement noise.  
   **Honesty:** show conditional detection probability across assumed slopes, noise and persistence, with simulation uncertainty—not “NASA will prove this by 2034.”  
   **Data:** noise characteristics from POWER T2M or IMERG, plus explicitly synthetic simulations.  
   **Awards:** Science, Inspirational. **Hours:** 4–6. **Risk:** false precision from uncertain noise models.

9. **Classroom Jury: measure whether investigating changes reasoning.**  
   **Wow:** learners commit to a conclusion, cross-examine evidence, rewrite the claim, then solve a different transfer question.  
   **Honesty:** assess reasoning rather than agreement with a preferred conclusion; report actual participant counts and results without generalizing from a tiny pilot.  
   **Data:** existing Alaska and Sahel cases.  
   **Awards:** Inspirational, Galactic Impact, Storytelling. **Hours:** 1.5–2, plus owner-led testing. **Risk:** shipping a quiz without demonstrating learning.

10. **The Missing-Water Score: hear rainfall and stored water diverge.**  
    **Wow:** rainfall pulses continue while a sustained water-storage tone descends; captions explain that rain and stored water answer different questions.  
    **Honesty:** publish the sound mapping, preserve gaps, provide a silent equivalent; GRACE measures total storage anomalies, not groundwater alone or time until depletion.  
    **Data:** IMERG V07 and [JPL GRACE/GRACE-FO RL06.3_v04 mascons](https://grace.jpl.nasa.gov/data/get-data/jpl_global_mascons/), aggregated over a large basin.  
    **Awards:** Art and Technology, Storytelling, Data. **Hours:** 6–9. **Risk:** acquisition and scientifically misleading musical drama.

**3. The 14-hour combination**

**Build the Headline Clinic around the opposite-headline experiment.** Attach receipts, demonstrate learning, and add a second data witness if time holds. Keep the planet already underway. Defer the atlas, additional missions and sonification.

This budget assumes the existing validity repairs and planet have separate owners; it does **not** absorb the unresolved statistical-validation workload.

The owner should recruit one educator and several adult learners now; agents can prepare the exercise, but cannot manufacture user evidence.

**4. Predicted scores**

**25/25 is an attainable ceiling. My planning forecast is 23/25**, with two evidence-dependent opportunities to raise it:

| Criterion | Forecast | Requirement for 5 |
|---|---:|---|
| Impact | 4 | Demonstrated reasoning improvement and a usable educator deployment |
| Creativity | 5 | Visitors construct, expose and repair a misleading claim themselves |
| Validity | 4 | Complete-procedure calibration, faithful displays and enforced withholding |
| Relevance | 5 | Working five-step investigation with traceable NASA evidence |
| Presentation | 5 | Finished narrative and equally clear public product |

**Presentation 5** requires the opening contradiction to register immediately, readable dates and units, captions, a reliable cached path, and a phone experience that matches the video.

**Creativity 5** requires an interaction that changes the visitor’s thinking. Constructing an opposite headline, discovering why it misleads, and publishing a reproducible correction delivers that transformation.

Prioritize **Best Storytelling, Most Inspirational and Best Use of Science**. Witness comparison strengthens **Best Use of Data**. Galactic Impact needs demonstrated reuse; Art and Technology needs meaningful artistic execution beyond the globe.
