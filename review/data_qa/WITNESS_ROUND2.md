## Task 1. C-A: station-based air-temperature witness (BUILT)

Output: `web/data/witness/C-A_knmi.json`. Script: `data/witness_knmi/make_knmi_ca_witness.py` (stats venv, `-I`). Raw text copies: `data/witness_knmi/C-A_cru_ts410_tmx_raw.txt` (main) and `C-A_cru_ts410_tmp_raw.txt` (sensitivity).

### Provenance
- KNMI Climate Explorer, CRU TS 4.10 maximum temperature (tmx), 0.5 degree, land points only (CRU land/sea mask, more than 50 percent land, at least 30 percent valid), average over lat 30-37N, lon 90W-75.5W. Monthly degC, 1901-2025; rows 2001-2025 saved. JJA = mean of Jun, Jul, Aug.
- Page: https://climexp.knmi.nl/data/icru4_tmx_-90--75.5E_30-37N_firstyear-lastyear_n_5lan.dat (run stamp 2026-11-14 20:56:29). Generated by a plain HTTP GET of `get_index.cgi?...&field=cru4_tmx&lat1=30&lat2=37&lon1=-90&lon2=-75.5&masktype=5lan&minfac=30&email=someone@somewhere`; no login, no download, no browser needed.
- Sensitivity: tmp (mean temperature), run stamp 2026-11-14 20:57:13, same box.
- Citation: KNMI Climate Explorer; CRU TS 4.10, Harris et al. 2020 / University of East Anglia CRU.
- Sensors: none. Built from weather stations only, so it shares no input with MODIS or the ESA LST record. It measures a different quantity.

### Quantity warning
CRU tmx is the monthly mean of daily maximum air temperature at 2 m. The case (MODIS Terra) and ESA LST are land surface (skin) temperature at the satellite overpass. Levels are not compared; only trends and year-to-year changes. CRU tmx is derived from mean temperature and diurnal range, and the range comes from fewer stations.

No tmx or ESA trend is significant. Only the mean-temperature (tmp) sensitivity series is (p 0.014 Hamed-Rao, 0.031 Yue-Wang); the gate says the regional tests are too liberal at 25 seasons, so read that with caution.

Agreement of anomalies (Pearson r; r of year-to-year changes in brackets):
- KNMI vs MODIS registered: r 0.79 (0.92).
- KNMI vs ESA: r 0.89 (0.89).
- KNMI vs MODIS, ESA footprint: r 0.83 (0.91).
So the year-to-year behaviour of all three agrees closely; they differ in trend.

Difference series (KNMI minus satellite, anomaly differences):
- KNMI minus MODIS registered: Sen +0.62/decade (CI +0.39, +0.87), p Hamed-Rao 1e-5, Yue-Wang 7e-5.
- KNMI minus MODIS, ESA footprint: +0.54/decade, p 3e-5.
- KNMI minus ESA: +0.005/decade (CI -0.37, +0.41), p 1.0. No trend in the difference.
So ESA tracks the station series; MODIS Terra drifts colder relative to it.

### Does the divergence start around 2021?
Not as a clean break. MODIS minus air temperature already trends before 2021: KNMI minus MODIS over 2001-2020 is +0.53/decade (CI +0.24, +0.90, p 0.003). It gets worse at the end.
- Regress MODIS on KNMI over 2001-2020 (slope 0.92, residual SD 0.48 degC). Residuals 2021-2025: -0.44, -0.48, -0.69, -0.92, -1.51 (mean -0.81, or -1.7 SD). Growing each year; 2025 is the largest.
- Same on the ESA footprint, 2021-2024: -0.43, -0.48, -0.78, -0.94.
- ESA on KNMI, 2021-2024: +0.36, +0.24, +0.02, +0.29 (mean +0.23, +0.4 SD): no drift.
- Mean KNMI minus MODIS: 2001-2020 -0.17, 2021-2025 +0.66; shift +0.83 (Welch p 0.009, permutation p 0.002; indicative only, 5 late years, autocorrelated).
- Mean KNMI minus ESA: shift -0.27 (permutation p 0.36): nothing.
Caveat: ESA uses Terra MODIS in 2012-2018, so it is not independent of Terra then; in 2021-2024 it uses Sentinel-3B.

### Conclusion for the docket
Station-based summer maximum air temperature and the ESA multi-sensor record both lean upward (+0.3 to +0.4 degC per decade, not significant), the registered MODIS Terra series leans downward (-0.35), the three agree on year-to-year changes (r 0.8 to 0.9), and the gap between MODIS and the other two grows through the early 2020s, so this witness gives no support for reading the MODIS cooling as real; the C-A verdict stays withheld. No causal claim is made about why.

The C-D period is 2003-2025 (23 JJA seasons). The best single product (AATSR_ENS, or SU/ADV) covers full JJA only for 2003-2011, which is 9 seasons, and nothing at all for 2012-2025. The rule was at least 15 seasons without a multi-year gap; 9 fails, and no other product here bridges 2012-2025. Stopped; no data were downloaded.

Option noted, not built: KNMI Climate Explorer (reanalysis page, no login) lists NASA MERRA-2 with surface, 2 m/10 m and pressure-level variables only; no AOD variable is exposed there and no CAMS reanalysis was found. MERRA-2 assimilates MODIS AOD, so it would not be independent of the C-D case anyway. The observations page offers only TOMS aerosol index (1979-2005) and GACP AOD over the sea (1981-2009), neither usable. A later C3S SLSTR/ATSR aerosol product (Copernicus Climate Data Store, not CEDA) was not checked and is outside this brief.
