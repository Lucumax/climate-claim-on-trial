Visual modules lab

Five views of the same case, each answering one question. Descriptive only: no trend, test or verdict is computed here. Not linked from the site.

Globe

Where is it changing? The real cells of the case, year by year, on the planet.

Anomaly map

Where, and when? The same cells on a flat map with a fixed colour range, so years can be compared by eye.

Spiral

How did each season change over time? Every month of the region, loop by loop, around the baseline ring.

Distribution

Is the whole distribution moving, not just the average? All cells and years in each period, side by side.

Stripes

What does the regional average look like, year after year? One stripe per year; optional header strip.

Tornado

How did the whole year change, loop on loop? One ring per year, stacked with the oldest at the bottom; a wider ring means warmer months. Needs a full 12-month record for rings; seasonal cases show short arcs at their real calendar angles.
